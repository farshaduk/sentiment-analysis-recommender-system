COMMON FAILURE PATTERNS
============================================================
  • Sarcasm / irony — lexicons take words at face value.
  • Mixed sentiment — 'Great product but shipping was awful.'
  • Short / vague reviews — too little text for reliable scoring.
  • Neutral reviews misclassified — hardest class for both models
    because neutral language overlaps with weak positive/negative.
